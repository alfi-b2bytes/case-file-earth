# Learn along

A running log of what the team learns during NASA Space Apps 2026. Newest first. Only what we actually did and saw.

## 14 Nov 2026

- The challenge asks for four things: pick variables, get regional data, plot a long time series, and test whether the change is significant.
- Official rules we are building around (Space Apps Project Submission Guide and Judging & Awards Guide, read 14 Nov 2026): the demo is a video of 30 seconds or less, or a deck of 7 slides or less; submission material is in English; at least one NASA data source is used and cited; AI use is declared.
- Presentation is one of the five judging criteria, so the story gets as much care as the code.
- Space Apps terms do not accept material that promotes commercial services or products, so this project is a free, open tool.
- Live NASA tools can fail. We plan to rely on the NASA POWER API plus a frozen offline copy of the demo data.
